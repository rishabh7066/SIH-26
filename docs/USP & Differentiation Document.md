# USP & Differentiation Document
## SIH Problem Statement 26091
### AI-Driven Hyper-Local Business Advisory and Financial Structuring Assistant for Rural Micro-Entrepreneurs

---

## 1. Executive USP

### **“Before taking a loan, know whether the business can actually work in your village.”**

The proposed solution is not merely a loan calculator, chatbot, or business-plan generator.

It is an **AI-powered hyper-local decision intelligence platform** that combines:

- Village-level market intelligence
- Competitor density analysis
- Local demand estimation
- Business opportunity-gap detection
- Localized pricing intelligence
- Supply-chain and seasonal risk analysis
- Financial feasibility
- Scheme eligibility
- Loan structuring
- Working-capital planning
- Explainable AI recommendations

The key differentiation is that the system connects **“Where you want to start” + “What you want to start” + “How much money you have”** into one decision.

---

# 2. Core USP

## USP 1 — Hyper-Local Business Viability Engine

### Problem

Existing business advisory systems generally provide generic advice:

> “Dairy is a profitable business.”

But a business can be profitable in one village and fail in another.

### Proposed Innovation

The system evaluates a proposed business against the **specific local economic ecosystem**.

Input:

> Village + Block + District + Business Category + Available Capital

Output:

> **Business Viability Score**

Example:

### Dairy Business
**78/100 — Recommended with Conditions**

The score is calculated using multiple dimensions:

- Local demand
- Competition
- Consumer density
- Purchasing power
- Accessibility
- Supply availability
- Market proximity
- Seasonal risk
- Estimated margins
- Financial feasibility

### Why it is unique

The recommendation changes according to the location.

The system is therefore not answering:

> “Is dairy a good business?”

It is answering:

> **“Is dairy a good business HERE, with THIS budget, under THESE local conditions?”**

# 5. USP 4 — “Margin-to-Business” Financial Reverse Planner

Most loan calculators work in the forward direction:

> Loan → EMI

Our system works in both directions.

### Forward Planning

User:

> “I have ₹1 lakh.”

System:

> Project Cost ≈ ₹10 lakh  
> Loan requirement ≈ ₹9 lakh

### Reverse Planning

User:

> “I can invest only ₹1 lakh and don't want my repayment burden to exceed my projected cash flow.”

System evaluates:

- Maximum practical project size
- Loan requirement
- Estimated repayment
- Business operating costs
- Working capital
- Expected revenue

Then determines whether the proposed business is financially sustainable.

### Key USP

The system distinguishes between:

**Maximum Loan Eligibility**

and

**Recommended Loan Amount**

These are NOT always the same.

Example:

```text
Maximum eligible loan: ₹9,00,000

Recommended loan: ₹7,20,000

Reason:
Projected business cash flow cannot comfortably
support the maximum borrowing.
```

This prevents users from borrowing simply because they are eligible.

# 6. USP 5 — “Loan Eligibility ≠ Loan Affordability” Engine

This is a major financial-literacy feature.

A beneficiary may technically qualify for:

> ₹9 lakh loan

But the business may not generate enough cash flow to comfortably service that debt.

The platform therefore produces two indicators:

### Eligibility Score
**92/100**

and

### Affordability Score
**71/100**

### AI Explanation

> “You may be eligible for the requested financing, but the projected business cash flow suggests that taking the maximum loan may create repayment pressure.”

This turns the platform from a **loan-enabling tool into a responsible borrowing advisor**.

# 26. USP 25 — Separate AI Layer and Deterministic Financial Engine

This is an important technical USP.

Financial calculations should NOT depend on an LLM.

Architecture:

```text
                USER
                  │
        ┌─────────┴─────────┐
        ↓                   ↓
  AI Advisory Engine   Financial Engine
        │                   │
        ↓                   ↓
 Market Analysis      Exact Calculations
 SWOT                 Loan
 Opportunities        EMI
 Risks                Scheme
 Pricing              Cash Flow
        │                   │
        └─────────┬─────────┘
                  ↓
             AI REPORT
```

The AI interprets results, while the financial engine performs deterministic calculations.

This improves:

- Accuracy
- Auditability
- Trust
- Explainability
