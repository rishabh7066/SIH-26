# GramVenture AI — New Features

## 1. AI Business Finder
**Purpose:** Recommend the best businesses for a specific village.

- Village / Location
- Budget
- Existing business
- Skills / Interests
- Available resources
- Top business recommendations
- Demand score
- Competition score
- Investment required
- Expected profit
- Risk level

---

## 2. Village Business Gap Map
**Purpose:** Identify missing or underserved businesses in a village.

- Interactive village map
- Existing shops/businesses
- Business density
- Missing business categories
- Nearby village comparison
- AI opportunity recommendations

**USP:** Shows what the village needs instead of simply suggesting generic businesses.

---

## 3. Business Opportunity Score
**Purpose:** Give every business idea an overall opportunity score.

### Score Factors
- Local Demand
- Competition
- Profit Potential
- Investment
- Local Resources
- Customer Reach
- Risk

**Output:** Opportunity Score out of 100.

---

## 4. Budget Business Planner
**Purpose:** Suggest businesses according to available capital.

### Example
- ₹25,000
- ₹50,000
- ₹1,00,000
- ₹5,00,000+

### Output
- Suitable businesses
- Initial investment
- Monthly expenses
- Expected revenue
- Expected profit
- Break-even period
- Risk level

---

## 5. Business Simulator
**Purpose:** Simulate business performance before investing.

### Adjustable Inputs
- Investment
- Customers per day
- Average selling price
- Rent
- Employee cost
- Product cost
- Other expenses

### Output
- Monthly revenue
- Monthly expenses
- Monthly profit
- Break-even time
- Profit margin
- Risk level

### AI What-If Analysis
Examples:
- What if customers decrease by 20%?
- What if rent increases?
- What if product prices increase?

---

## 6. Loan Readiness & Safe EMI
**Purpose:** Help users decide how much loan they can safely afford.

### Features
- Loan amount calculator
- EMI calculator
- Interest rate
- Loan tenure
- Expected business profit
- Safe EMI limit
- Loan readiness score
- Repayment risk indicator

**USP:** Recommends how much loan the user *should take*, not just how much they *can get*.

---

## 7. Government Scheme Matcher
**Purpose:** Match users with relevant government schemes.

### User Profile
- Age
- Location
- Business type
- Investment
- Eligibility details

### Output
- Matching schemes
- Eligibility
- Benefits
- Required documents
- Application guidance
- Match percentage

---

## 8. Voice AI Rural Advisor
**Purpose:** Allow users to interact with GramVenture AI using voice.

### Features
- Hindi voice input
- Regional/local language support
- Voice answers
- Business recommendations
- Voice-based financial guidance
- Simple conversational interface

**Example:**  
“मेरे पास एक लाख रुपये हैं और मेरे गाँव में 2000 लोग हैं, कौन सा बिज़नेस शुरू करूँ?”
